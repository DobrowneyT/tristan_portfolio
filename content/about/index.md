Hi, I'm Tristan, a systems engineer in Winnipeg.

At [PTx Trimble](https://ptxtrimble.com/) I work on OutRun, a platform that makes tractors autonomous. Most days that means integrating sensors, cameras and controllers into one system, and it can take me anywhere in the stack: a wiring harness, STM32 firmware, our custom Linux kernel on NVIDIA Jetson, or the ROS2 applications on top. I like owning a system end to end.

I studied Computer Engineering at the University of Manitoba (B.Sc., With Distinction). I finished my courses in December 2025, and the degree was conferred in February 2026. Along the way I did co-op terms in controls and embedded design, built PCBs for magnetometer research in the Electromagnetic Imaging Lab, and spent my capstone year teaching a robot to play the piano.

Before engineering, I spent twelve years as a dancer with Canada's Royal Winnipeg Ballet, finishing as First Soloist. That taught me how to practise something until it works, which turns out to be most of debugging.
