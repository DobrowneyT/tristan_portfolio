For my undergraduate research term I designed the electronics for a three-channel magnetometer used in the lab's research. That meant power regulation, signal conditioning, analogue-to-digital conversion and a Raspberry Pi 5 carrier board, taken from prototype boards through to the final revision. I also wrote the C++ that reads the converter and its supporting sensors.

More detail is coming once I've confirmed with the lab what can be published.
